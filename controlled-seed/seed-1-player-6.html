Controlled CivBench on Vox Populi 5.2.7, Civilization V

Seed 1 · Player 6A controlled version of CivBench (Chen et al., 2026). Each LLM strategists rotate through 3 standard maps (8 players), each game features 2 of the same LLM strategists + 6 Vox Populi AI players. Experimented with 2 conditions: having LLMs make a decision every turn (base); having them make a decision every 5 turns, or when an important event comes up (per-5). baseline_experiment: vanilla-standard-fixed; estimator: attention; strength_table: strength
